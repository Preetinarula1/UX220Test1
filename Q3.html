Tim Berners-Lee and the World Wide Web

Original Research

Tim Berners-Lee worked at CERN, where scientist studied physics. He created the Word Wide Web to help researchers share information and documents.

Today's World Wide Web

Today, people use the web for shopping, education, entertainment, communication, and social media.

Comparison

Originally, the web helped scientists share research. Today, it connects people around the world for many different purposes. Both uses focus on sharing and accessing information.
